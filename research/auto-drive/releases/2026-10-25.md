# AutoDrive research preview — 2026-10-25

This is a research preview of turn-boundary continuation control for coding agents, built on the OpenCode fork. It is not a production-stability certification, a completed benchmark study, or a published journal/arXiv paper.

## Included in this update

- Seventeen implementation and research commits after `0b13669e066e340960836d570b6cbddb45b20213`, through `bd28b931d183859b9e2ed383968baa9730e4b8b1`.
- Annotation accounting and terminal-output reconciliation fixes, including precision-preserving budget checks.
- Ablation-runner failure preservation and actual CLI regression coverage.
- Corrected paired-outcome analysis, uncertainty intervals, comparison-family correction, and result-export validation.
- Offline study-design sensitivity calculations, explicitly identified as hypothetical rather than measured results.
- Revised manuscript, appendix, references, methodology audits, and two author-placeholder/anonymous PDF drafts.

The implementation uses `continue`, `stop`, and `defer` decisions at turn boundaries, with bounded continuation, durable admission, and user-input priority. Exactly-once admission must not be interpreted as exactly-once model execution or tool side effects.
