# AutoDrive journal literature and submission audit

Audit date: 2026-10-03 (Asia/Shanghai). Scope: the core references and scientific positioning in `paper/main.tex` and `paper/references.bib`, plus first-party journal and methodological guidance. This is a literature audit, not an experiment result or an acceptance prediction. No experimental settings or labels were changed.

## Assessment

The five references singled out for verification are real, retrievable primary sources and match their cited titles and author lists. They should not be removed on suspicion alone. The larger weakness is a missing close comparator: **SCATE** explicitly addresses premature termination of coding agents through adaptive supervision. Accordingly, neither the existence of the failure nor the idea of automating a human's continuation intervention is a defensible first-of-its-kind claim. AutoDrive's plausible contribution is the narrower combination of a session-boundary intervention, separate defer semantics, durable admission, human-input priority, and a measured benefit under matched resource limits. That combination still needs empirical and mechanism evidence; novelty is not established merely by enumerating implementation features. [SCATE](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08983v1)

The current manuscript correctly calls itself a pre-results draft. Increasing its length or changing its template cannot establish a journal-level empirical contribution while its confirmatory results remain pending.

A native worker with an equal total turn/token budget is also a crucial control. Otherwise, an improvement from automatic follow-ups may reflect granting more inference budget rather than superior boundary decisions. This control is an inference from the intervention design, not a borrowed result. Keep native termination, imposed segment truncation, budget exhaustion, and controller stops distinct. A six-step artificial cap should not be counted as spontaneous conversational handoff.

The existing 384-run matrix is frozen. Add any new comparator or dataset through a separate dated supplement with its own estimand, run manifest, budget, and provenance; do not silently redefine the current confirmatory matrix or exceed the authorized total budget.

No verified instruction here says that 384 runs, a particular p-value, or any amount of editing guarantees acceptance. The following are reviewer-oriented recommendations: complete paired results; a budget-matched native baseline; a close published comparator; independent human validation of a prespecified boundary subset; leave-component-out ablations alongside cumulative additions; sensitivity to segment length; and independent artifact exercise. Human validation is especially important if claiming that the controller preserves user authority: agreement between language models alone is not a human ground truth.

## Concrete manuscript edits

1. Add SCATE to both the introduction and a mechanism-level related-work table. Avoid claiming novelty for automated continuation supervision or for identifying premature termination.
2. Introduce the operational event precisely: a worker-selected terminal response while an authorized, feasible next action remains. State whether externally imposed segment limits are included in each reported estimate.
3. Frame the expected contribution as an evaluated design trade-off: how much completion benefit remains after matching resources, and what defer errors, extra cost, and admission-recovery guarantees accompany it.
4. Preserve the distinction between exactly-once **admission** and exactly-once provider/tool execution. Link any theorem or invariant to its explicit transaction, uniqueness, and recovery assumptions.
5. Report model-panel labels as model-panel labels. Any human audit should have a prespecified sample, rubric, independent labels, disagreements, adjudication, and provenance; do not retrospectively call model agreement human validation.
6. Attribute SWE-bench Pro audit claims narrowly and add independent SWE-EVO validity checks. Avoid suggesting that rejecting one benchmark proves the chosen benchmark is sound.
7. Add a compact artifact inventory linking every empirical claim to a result file, generating script, input manifest, and integrity digest. Label unavailable remote evidence explicitly.
8. Keep publication readiness separate from the study plan: pending results, unresolved source access, author placeholders, and journal-specific formatting checks remain open gates.

Suggested related-work paragraph (adapt citations to the manuscript):

> Prior work already studies premature termination and automated supervision of coding agents. SCATE learns coverage- and cost-sensitive interventions for test generation, while Magentic-One maintains task and progress ledgers for multi-agent control. AutoDrive investigates a narrower integration point: a terminal worker turn within one admitted coding session. Its intended distinction is a defer-aware continuation policy coupled to durable admission and user-priority scheduling. Whether that integration yields useful improvements over resource-matched and evidence-aware controls is an empirical question; the current protocol does not establish those improvements in advance.

This paragraph is a synthesis of the sources above, not a quotation from them. It deliberately claims neither priority nor measured superiority.
