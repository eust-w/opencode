## 2. Agreement gate takes precedence over older annotation guidance

`annotations/guidelines.md` step 4 suggests clarifying rules and reannotation when κ < 0.75. That procedure is superseded for this frozen model-panel study by the **2026-08-31 autonomous reference-standard amendment**, which requires stopping and reporting RQ3 unavailable after failed agreement or class coverage. Do not repeat labels, tune prompts, select a better-agreeing panel, or inspect test labels to repair the gate. This correction does not change any label. Independent calibration and human annotation can be designed prospectively in a separate study.

The schema term `independent-model-panel` means separate blinded requests. It does not mean statistically independent judges, independent training data, or human gold. The availability fallback can include the controller itself. Report exact identities, role overlap, coverage, pre-adjudication agreement, adjudication, missingness, and exclusions.

## 3. Correct primary contrast and multiplicity

The paper's primary contrast is supervisor versus its **own** first-boundary prefix on the 48 primary repeat-zero tasks. Earlier code computed only oracle, blind, and regex comparisons, and its `SupervisorDelta` macro referred to regex. The corrected analysis emits the own-prefix contrast explicitly and separates the regex effect. It applies Holm to the four two-sided resolved-outcome tests together. Retain raw p-values and all effect estimates, including unfavorable ones.

This is a disclosed analysis-code correction. It does not turn an old three-comparison output into a four-comparison output, nor make the first-boundary comparison resource-matched. Any previously generated analysis must remain identified by its original code hash; a recomputation must use a new derived snapshot.

## 4. Execution success is not identical to patch success

The host executor can retain a passing final patch grade even when the execution terminates with timeout or budget failure. Operational resolution requires `status === "succeeded" && resolved`. The corrected formal analyzer preserves `rawResolved` and raw Fix Rate separately. It never changes the source record to make the grade match execution status. The off endpoint is the independently graded first-boundary patch, not an inferred final status.

Charged harness exclusions still require a complete disposition audit. The existing complete-matrix analyzer rejects missing rows; it is **not** an intention-to-treat analysis of all planned runs. Budget-overrun exclusions cannot disappear from the denominator of a claimed all-task study. Do not fabricate valid trajectory rows for them; retain raw exclusions, costs, and outcome-availability bounds in a distinct report.

## 5. Unsupported operational fields

The current automated executor initializes manual continuation and unsafe-continuation counts to zero and derives `recoverySucceeded` from ordinary run completion. These raw fields are preserved for schema compatibility, but none estimates human effort saved, absence of unauthorized actions, or injected-fault recovery. The paper renderer keeps manual-effort and recovery results pending regardless of those fields. Boundary STOP/DEFER false-continuation rates describe agreement errors against the reference, not actual executed harms.

`redundantTurns` counts consecutive equal patch hashes. Derived prose calls it the **unchanged-patch proxy**. Useful testing or diagnosis may leave a patch unchanged; semantic redundancy must not be inferred without action evidence.

## 6. Frequency, sample units, and availability

The sixth worker step explicitly requests a terminal summary and disables tools. RQ1 therefore concerns the imposed six-step interface, not natural termination prevalence. A frequency calculated on labeled first boundaries has an available-case denominator; publish labeled/planned/missing counts and worst-case missing-outcome bounds. Balanced RQ3 examples cannot estimate prevalence.

Trajectory-grouped development/test splitting prevents adjacent-history leakage, not same-task repetition across the split. Extra worker repeats are not independent new tasks. Of the 48 tasks, 26 are DVC; task-bootstrap intervals do not establish repository-population generalization.
