# AutoDrive Chinese introduction — approved design

The user approved the recommended approximately 90-second Chinese narration and subtitle concept, landscape and portrait exports, and adaptation for Bilibili, Douyin, WeChat Channels, Xiaohongshu, YouTube and X on 2026-10-25. Specific publishing accounts are still to be confirmed.

## Direction

A restrained technical explainer: warm ivory typography, dark navy background, mint continuation signal, coral stop signal, amber defer signal. Open with the familiar "continue?" interruption, explain the controller rather than pretending to show live benchmark success, animate the three decisions and durable queue, and close with the public research artifact and its incomplete evidence.

Original diagrams and typeset code are labelled mechanism illustrations. No fake terminal results, fabricated metrics, server addresses, private logs, screenshots of account data, or claims of journal/arXiv publication. Narration is synthetic and disclosed; no voice cloning or unlicensed music. A locally runnable, redistributable TTS option must be verified before generating public narration.

## Outputs

- Approximately 90 seconds (target 80–110 seconds), H.264/AAC MP4, 30 fps.
- 1920×1080 landscape and 1080×1920 portrait compositions, designed separately rather than cropped.
- Legible burned-in Chinese subtitles, separate SRT, WAV narration, and landscape/portrait covers.
- Versioned narration, renderer, timing data, factual claims map, reproducible build instructions and verification report.
- Platform-specific titles/descriptions/tags and a truthful publication ledger distinguishing prepared, uploaded, submitted, processing and publicly verified.

## Acceptance

Check every scene and subtitle for overflow and meaningful overlap; validate codecs, dimensions, duration, audio presence and clipping; listen/check narration rather than inferring success from file creation. Verify scientific statements against the repository. Use only confirmed accounts. Never solve login/CAPTCHA by bypass, and do not mark a platform published without observing its resulting state.
